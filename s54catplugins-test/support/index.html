Installing on macOS
  1. Download the file from your purchase and unzip it.
  2. Copy the .vst3 file into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and the .component file into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
  3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
  4. If macOS says the plugin can't be opened, right-click it, choose Open, and confirm.
Where is my download?

Log in to Patreon with the account you bought with and open your Purchases tab. Your files unlock there right after payment. Still nothing? Write to hello@s54catplugins.com.

Refunds

Refunds follow the policy of Patreon shown at checkout. If something doesn't work on your system, write to us first and we'll sort it out.

Which DAWs does it work with?

Any host that supports VST3 or AU plugins on macOS, such as Ableton Live, Logic Pro and Cubase.
