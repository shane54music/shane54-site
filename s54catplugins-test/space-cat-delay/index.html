What's inside

CUTOFF

  • Filter characters — switch between ORBITRON, HELIX and more
  • Cat-head cutoff that turns with the filter
  • NOIZ LVL — the hiss and grit that make it breathe
  • KEY OFF option

DELAY

  • TIME and FEEDBACK that run all the way into self-oscillation
  • TAP tempo and host SYNC
  • OVERFEED, SHUSH! and LATCH for playing the delay live
  • TAIL BARS — decide how long the echoes ring, ready to bounce
  • TONE control for the repeats

SCULPT

  • ENV — pluck the echoes with an envelope shape
  • EBB/FLO — a sweeping, breathing movement
  • Synced RATE controls for both

SPRING

  • Reverb types TANK and CLOUD 9
  • MIX, DECAY and COLOR
  • POST / LOOP placement
  • DUCK — the reverb gets out of the way of your signal

Questions

Is it a clean, everyday delay?

No, and it isn't trying to be. It's built for character: noise, self-oscillation, a filter that bites. Your stock delays cover the clean jobs.
