Payments

Purchases are handled by Patreon, an independent platform that processes the payment and, where required by law, collects taxes. We never see or store your card details, and the provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to your purchase.

Analytics (optional)

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are visited and which buttons are clicked, so we can improve the site. It is switched off until you press Accept in the cookie banner. If you press Reject or ignore the banner, no analytics script is loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

What is collected: pages viewed, clicks on buttons and links, scroll depth, approximate location (country/city), device, browser and language. We do not collect your name or email through analytics, and we turned off Google's advertising features and signals. Provider: Google Ireland Limited; Google may process data outside the EU/EEA under the safeguards it provides. Analytics data is kept for 2 months.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer. Details are on the cookie policy page.

Email and mailing list

If you write to us, we use your email address only to answer you. If you sign up for the mailing list, we store your email address with our email service provider, MailerLite, and use it only to send the news you asked for. Legal basis: your consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to us to be removed. We do not add buyers to the mailing list unless they sign up themselves.

Fonts

Typefaces are served from this website itself, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw any consent at any time, by writing to hello@s54catplugins.com. You can also complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH, naih.hu) or to the authority in your own country.
